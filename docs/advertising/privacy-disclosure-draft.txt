Advertising disclosure preparation — NOT PUBLISHED; not a statement that ads are live

Current status: Public KiwiKind website and distributed Android release do not
serve advertising. A separate internal test build uses official Google test IDs.

Draft for review before a future advertising release:

Advertising will be limited to clearly labelled placements on public information
pages. Sign-in, account, support, billing, privacy and deletion screens will remain
free of advertising. Advertising is optional to the operation of your KiwiKind
account; refusing optional advertising consent must not block essential account
functions.

If approved and enabled, Google advertising technology may process IP address and
general location, device identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information
to deliver and measure advertising and detect fraud. Website advertising may use
cookies or similar local storage. Advertising ID permission being absent does not
mean the advertising SDK processes no information. Personalised advertising or
optional storage must be requested only when the applicable consent requirements
are met. Google processes data according to its advertising and privacy policies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Applicable consent choices will appear before requests where required. A persistent
Advertising privacy choices control must allow users to revisit or withdraw choices.
Essential authentication cookies remain separate from optional advertising storage.
Contact kiwikindbroadband@gmail.com for questions or account privacy requests.

Before publishing this draft:
- Confirm actual SDK/version, ad personalisation settings, regional messages, data
  purposes, collection/sharing and retention; disclose only verified behaviour.
- Configure and verify required Google-certified website CMP / Android UMP messages,
  including privacy options and withdrawal; test refusal and reset choices.
- Identify actual recipients, overseas processing, retention/deletion arrangements,
  and any applicable opt-out rights. Do not invent retention periods.
- Update Google Play declarations for the final distributed binary.
- Obtain app/site approval and explicit owner authorisation for live advertising.
- Preserve the existing accurate prelaunch and not-incorporated status.

This draft is not a substitute for configuring consent. No live Google script or
production advertising ID has been added to the website/app by preparing it.
