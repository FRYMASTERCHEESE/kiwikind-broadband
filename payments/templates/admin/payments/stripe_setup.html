Before taking real money

Complete provider checkout, renewal, failed-payment, cancellation, refund and webhook-replay tests. Confirm account activation and payouts. Review the signed Android build and customer terms. Then explicitly approve KIWIKIND_LIVE_RELEASE_APPROVED=1 and KIWIKIND_LIVE_BILLING_ENABLED=1. Both default off. Do not enable them just to make a button appear.

Public plan publication and supplier ordering are independent. No service is automatically ordered from Vetta. Only a verified-owner-approved customer agreement can be paid, and only after its approved payment-start date.
