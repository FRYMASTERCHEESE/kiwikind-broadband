Manual partner portal checklist

  1. Confirm agreements, prices, GST treatment, support processes, privacy and retail terms before handling a live order.
  2. Open the genuine partner portal using the link and account Vetta supplies. This system stores no supplier password and has no ordering API or sandbox.
  3. Check the exact service address, record a reference and recheck deadline, and verify the selected speed tier and quote.
  4. After a future launch approval, obtain customer acceptance and switching authority before manually entering any order. Check the portal for an existing order before retrying.
  5. Record supplier status and references; manage first-line support, router supply and customer updates through KiwiKind.
  6. For faults, record diagnostics and line-test results before escalating through the portal. Verify resolution before telling the customer a fault is fixed.
